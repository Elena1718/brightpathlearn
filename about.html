About Me

Hi! I believe that good tutoring is more than giving someone the correct answer. It is about helping a learner understand the idea, practice it, and become comfortable doing it independently.

My tutoring style is friendly, organized, and patient. I encourage questions and adjust the pace when a topic needs more time.

My approach

1. Start with your goal

We identify the assignment, topic, skill, or study goal you want to improve.

2. Break it into steps

Large or confusing topics are divided into smaller ideas that are easier to work with.

3. Practice together

You try examples while I provide guidance, explanations, and feedback.

4. Review and repeat

We summarize the important points and identify what you should practice next.

Accessibility is important

I aim to make tutoring welcoming for adults with disabilities and learners with different communication or learning needs. If you need a different pace, readable materials, breaks, or another reasonable accommodation, mention it when you contact me so we can plan ahead.
